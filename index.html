Betting systems

A system is a rule for how much to bet next. Each one changes how your wins and losses are spread out; none changes the long-run cost of the game, about 5.3¢ for every $1 bet. A "unit" is whatever chip you pick. "Even-money" means Red, Black, Odd, Even, 1 to 18 or 19 to 36.
