Betting systems

A system is a rule for how much to bet next. Each one changes how your wins and losses are spread out; none changes the long-run cost of the game, about 5.3¢ for every $1 bet. A "unit" is whatever chip you pick. "Even-money" means Red, Black, Odd, Even, 1 to 18 or 19 to 36.

Simulator

Plays as many sessions as you ask for, each as many spins as you ask for, so you can see how a bet and a method behave before you play them. Tap a quick pick or type any number. Close this to change the chips on the simulator table, then press Simulate again.
